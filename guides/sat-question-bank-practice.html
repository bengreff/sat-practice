SAT Question Bank Practice by Difficulty (Bands 1–7)

The College Board's SAT Suite Question Bank holds about 3,800 official SAT questions, each labeled with its skill and difficulty. This site lets you practice all of them by difficulty band, section, domain and skill.

What is in the question bank

As of October 2026 the SAT part of the bank has 1,845 Reading and Writing questions and 1,925 Math questions. Every question has a difficulty band from 1 to 7: bands 1–3 are labeled Easy, 4–5 Medium and 6–7 Hard. Each also belongs to a domain and a skill:

Reading and WritingMath
Craft and Structure: Words in Context, Text Structure and Purpose, Cross-Text Connections Algebra: linear equations, linear functions, systems of linear equations, linear inequalities
Information and Ideas: Central Ideas and Details, Command of Evidence, Inferences Advanced Math: nonlinear functions, nonlinear equations and systems, equivalent expressions
Standard English Conventions: Boundaries; Form, Structure, and Sense Problem-Solving and Data Analysis: ratios and rates, percentages, data and statistics, probability
Expression of Ideas: Transitions, Rhetorical Synthesis Geometry and Trigonometry: area and volume, lines and angles, right triangles and trigonometry, circles

How practice works here

  • Choose sections, difficulty bands, domains and skills, each with a weight; or a preset such as Hardest (band 7), Hard (bands 6–7) or Mistakes & flags.
  • Questions you haven't seen come first, and you can cap how often any question repeats.
  • Check each answer instantly and read the College Board's explanation; add notes and flags.
  • Typed-in math answers are checked the way the SAT checks them, including equivalent fractions and decimals.
  • Not sure where to start? Enter your scores under Plan for practice aimed at your level and your weakest domains.
  • Stats shows your accuracy by band, domain and skill, and an estimated score.

The question bank and the Bluebook practice tests

About 2,000 of the bank's questions are also used on the College Board's Bluebook practice tests; the bank calls them "active". If you plan to take those practice tests, this site's tests can leave the active questions out so the Bluebook tests stay new to you.

Where the questions come from

Your browser downloads the questions straight from the College Board's question bank the first time you visit (about 20 minutes; the ones your practice settings ask for come first). This site doesn't store or host any question text.
